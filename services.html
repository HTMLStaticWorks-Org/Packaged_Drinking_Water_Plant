TREATMENT SYSTEMS

Water treatment, configured with purpose.

Source-water analysis guides the selection and sequence of treatment stages. The right system is specific to the water profile, required output and finished-product specification.

Treatment and bottling equipment planned as one connected system.

Start with the water. Then select the process.

Depending on source-water characteristics, the treatment train may include pre-filtration, activated carbon, cartridge filtration, membrane treatment and validated disinfection. Not every plant needs an identical sequence.

BOTTLING & PACKAGING

Protect the product through every hand-off.

Container preparation, rinsing, filling, capping, coding, labelling and secondary packaging should follow a clean, logical flow. Equipment, line speed and pack format are coordinated around the plant’s operating plan.

For larger jars, the workflow also includes container inspection, washing and sanitisation steps appropriate to the approved process.

QUALITY & DOCUMENTATION

Make the checks visible and reviewable.

Quality support includes sampling plans, in-process monitoring, finished-pack checks, sanitation records and batch documentation. The exact test panel and frequency must follow the applicable product category, current requirements and approved quality plan.

Clear records make it easier to review a result in context and trace it to the right batch.

WAREHOUSING & DISTRIBUTION

Order schedules, pack mix, stock rotation and dispatch windows are planned together to support smoother distributor and institutional supply.
