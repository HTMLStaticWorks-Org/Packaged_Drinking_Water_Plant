AquaVera website setup

Open index.html to view Home 1. Use the navigation to access Home 2, About, Services, Products, Quality Reports and Contact.

Keep the assets folder beside the HTML files. It contains the shared stylesheet, JavaScript and favicon.

Photographs are served from Pexels image CDN URLs and require an internet connection. The source register is in IMAGE_SOURCES.html.

The enquiry and newsletter forms are front-end demos and do not send or store submissions. Replace sample contact details and connect a real backend before launch.
