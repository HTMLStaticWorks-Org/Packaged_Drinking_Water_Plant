HOW WE WORK

A connected journey from raw water to finished pack.

Our operating model follows a clear sequence: assess incoming water, configure treatment to its profile, maintain protected storage, prepare containers, fill and seal under controlled conditions, then review batch and dispatch records.

The actual treatment train and monitoring plan must always be validated against the plant’s source water, product category and applicable requirements.
